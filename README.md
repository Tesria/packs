# Tesria Pack Library

Wiki packs made by Tesria users, reviewed and curated. A pack is a whole
[Tesria](https://github.com/Tesria/Tesria) space in one `.zip` file: pages,
pictures and structure, ready to import into any Tesria.

The library opens soon at [tesria.com/packs](https://tesria.com/packs/).
Submissions are open now.

## Submitting a pack

1. In your Tesria, export the space as a pack.
2. Open [Submit a pack](https://github.com/Tesria/packs/issues/new?template=submit-pack.yml)
   (or start from [tesria.com/packs](https://tesria.com/packs/)).
3. Fill in the name, a short description, a category and a license, and drag
   the `.zip` into the form.

## What happens next

Every submission is imported into a fresh Tesria and read before it's listed.
We check that:

- it imports cleanly and holds what the description says;
- it's the submitter's own work, or they have the right to share all of it,
  pictures included;
- it holds no one's personal details: no names, emails, addresses or phone
  numbers of real people who haven't agreed to be there;
- links and embeds go where they say.

Accepted packs are added under [`packs/`](packs/) and listed in the library.
If something needs changing, we'll say so on the issue.

## Licenses

Every pack is shared under one of these, chosen by its author:

- [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): reuse with credit
- [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): reuse with
  credit, and share changes the same way
- [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): public domain

## Importing a pack safely

Treat a pack like any file from the internet. Review makes problems unlikely,
not impossible: import into a space you can delete, and look before you share it.

## Reporting a problem with a listed pack

Open an issue naming the pack and what's wrong. If it holds personal details
that shouldn't be there, say where they are without repeating them, and the pack
comes down while it's looked at.
